# Make your first paid VM sale

A sale occurs when a simulated customer orders your published VM offer, the game
provisions its guest, and the prepaid payment reaches your balance. Publishing a
product, creating a free order or selling a dedicated physical server does not
complete this VM milestone.

## Wait while the business runs

Check that the Wiki storefront and product tasks are green. In **HOSTING → Market /
subscriptions**, enable a positive calendar speed and daily prospects. If you
paused time while building, resume it. The top-bar Speed multiplier can accelerate
waiting, but it also accelerates workloads, renewals and loan deadlines.

Customers arrive over time and compare your price, resources, quality and
reputation with alternatives. Watch expected daily orders; zero prospects, a
paused calendar, unavailable stock or an unattractive product can prevent a sale.
Keep the controller and node powered, and leave their networks connected.

Look in **Customer services** for the customer's running guest and in **Market /
subscriptions** for its payment. This is the guide's finish line. Its historical
completion remains after a VM is terminated, refunded or its product is deleted.

## Your next business decision

Now observe the real workload. Decide whether to improve service quality, attract
more customers, or add capacity. Revenue can fund more RAM, faster storage, better
uplinks or another node. Revisit these articles whenever you need to diagnose or
rebuild the network.
